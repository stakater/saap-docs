# Govern

Stakater uses a Risk and Compliance as Code (RCaC) approach to embed compliance practices directly into infrastructure and workflows, making compliance an automated, continuous process rather than a manual one.

{{ product_name }} gives your teams automated checks, auditable policies, and streamlined workflows to mitigate risks and maintain compliance with GDPR, NIST, ISO 27001, BSI IT-Grundschutz, and other standards.

The supported compliance frameworks fall into two categories.

---

## General frameworks

These frameworks apply across industries and provide high-level best practices for security, privacy, and risk management:

- [ISO 27001](iso27k1.md) — best practices for Information Security Management
- [NIST SP 800-171](nist-sp-800-171.md) — strengthening cybersecurity
- [GDPR](gdpr-eu.md) — data privacy and protection in the European Union
- [BSI IT-Grundschutz](bsi-it-grundschutz.md) — German Federal Office for Information Security standards
- [CIS Benchmarks](cis.md) — globally recognized secure configuration guidelines
- [SOC 2 Type 2](soc2-type2.md) — operational effectiveness of security and privacy controls

---

## Industry-specific standards

These standards address the compliance, security, and operational requirements of specific industries:

- [HIPAA](hipaa.md) — patient data protection
- [DORA](dora.md) — operational resilience in financial services
