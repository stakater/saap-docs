# Complior

Complior offers [compliant cloud](https://www.complior.se/compliance-platform/compliant-cloud/), and [private cloud](https://www.complior.se/compliance-platform/private-secure-cloud/), and {{ product_name }} can run on all of them.

The minimum quotas needed to be able to deploy {{ product_name }} in your account is listed on [Sizing of {{ product_name }}](../../for-administrators/plan-your-environment/sizing.md).

Complior is headquartered in Sweden.

If you are an existing Complior customer, email [`sales@stakater.com`](mailto:sales@stakater.com) to get a quote for {{ product_name }} in your account.

If you would like to become a Complior customer, [sign up](https://www.complior.se/compliance-platform/) for one of their clouds and email [`sales@stakater.com`](mailto:sales@stakater.com) to get {{ product_name }}.
